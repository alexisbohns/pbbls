## Verdict

The iOS surface carries genuinely careful engineering (no telemetry SDKs, typed RPC boundaries, tolerant cross-surface decoders, 451 well-built Swift Testing cases) wrapped around a set of ship-blocking gaps. The worst domain is Safety and Wellbeing (32, grade E): the app ships zero crisis resources and fires the same karma celebration haptic, ceramic sound, and achievement trophy over a maximum-negative entry as over a joyful one (SAF-ios-01), while rewards are conditioned on emotional disclosure (SAF-ios-03) and no age gate exists (SAF-ios-02). The strongest structural asset is Code Quality and Architecture (67, grade C, the surface's best): business rules are extracted into tested pure types and every multi-table write goes through a single transactional RPC. But the single highest-severity finding is that no CI gate runs that excellent test suite (TST-ios-01, severity 16), so every one of the 451 tests protects nothing at merge, and App Store submission is blocked today by a missing `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` manifest (GDP-ios-02 and PLT-ios-01, both P0). Federated Apple and Google signups create accounts with no consent record at all (GDP-ios-01), and sign-out leaves the previous user's intimate draft on disk for the next account to restore (PRV-ios-01). The surface scores 48 overall (D band), dragged down by Safety (E) and held up only by Architecture and Agentic Readiness (both C).

#### 🟠 F-2026-08-A11Y-ios-01 — The entire PebblesFont typography layer is fixed-size, so branded text ignores Dynamic Type across the app

- **Criterion:** A11Y-04 (Dynamic Type, font scaling, touch targets)
- **Priority:** P1 · **Cost:** M · **Impact × Likelihood:** 3 × 5 = 15 (High)
- **Where:** `apps/ios/Pebbles/Theme/Font+Pebbles.swift:105-127` (token-to-fixed-size map), `171-248` (helpers, no metrics scaling); grep `UIFontMetrics|relativeTo:` over `apps/ios/Pebbles` excluding `@ScaledMetric` = 0 hits; 81 pebblesFont call sites (grep count); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-16-android-parity-audit.md:147`.

- **Fix:** Route every family helper through `UIFontMetrics(forTextStyle:).scaledFont(for:)` (map each token to its nearest UIFont.TextStyle), or rebuild tokens on `Font.custom(_:size:relativeTo:)`; then walk the primary flows at the largest accessibility sizes and fix clipping (the valence lockup and record name input are the likely casualties).
> **Verification (CONFIRMED):** Independently re-read Font+Pebbles.swift: all token sizes are literal CGFloats and all six helpers build `Font(UIFont(descriptor:size:))` with no UIFontMetrics, which SwiftUI renders at fixed size regardless of Dynamic Type. Aggravating factor found: `@ScaledMetric`-driven container dimensions (52pt button heights, 44pt checkbox) do scale while the pebblesFont labels inside them do not, so the mismatch appears within single controls. Impact 3 × likelihood 5 is honest: deterministic for any non-default text size on nearly every screen.

#### 🟠 F-2026-08-PRV-ios-01 — Sign-out and account deletion leave the previous user's intimate content on device and serve it to the next account

- **Criterion:** PRV-06 (Local and offline data protection)
- **Priority:** P1 · **Cost:** M · **Impact × Likelihood:** 4 × 3 = 12 (High)
- **Where:** `apps/ios/Pebbles/Services/ComposerSnapshotStore.swift:29-33` (file location), `68-70` (clear only); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/ComposerDraftCoordinator.swift:74-105` (unconditional offerRestore); `apps/ios/Pebbles/RootView.swift:161-167` (sign-out clears only snapURLs); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Profile/Sheets/SettingsSheet.swift:404-416` (deletion path); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Services/ReferenceDataService.swift:22-24,41-81` (in-memory souls/collections, loaded once per launch at `RootView.swift:134-135`); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Record/Steps/RecordSoulsStep.swift:6,17` (reads refs.souls); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/Models/PebbleDraftPayload.swift:20-32` (payload fields).

> **Verification (CONFIRMED):** Re-read every cited file plus all callers. #### 🟡 F-2026-08-TST-cross-surface-01 — Android emits timestamps via OffsetDateTime.toString(), producing seconds-less ISO strings that iOS silently drops on draft resume

- **Criterion:** TST-02 (Shared shapes tested against real cross-surface payloads)
- **Priority:** P2 · **Cost:** M · **Impact × Likelihood:** 3 × 3 = 9 (Medium)
- **Where:** `apps/android/app/src/main/kotlin/app/pbbls/android/features/path/models/OffsetDateTimeSerializer.kt:29` (`encodeString(value.toString())`); `.../features/path/create/WhenDateTime.kt:36` (`date.atTime(hour, minute)`, seconds/nanos zero); `.../features/path/models/PebbleDraftPayload.kt:32-34`; `.../features/path/models/PebblePayloadTest.kt:118` ('toString() trims :00 seconds'); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/Models/ISO8601Flexible.swift:27-43` (fractional-then-whole-second only; neither accepts a seconds-less time); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/Models/PebbleDraftPayload.swift:88-93` and `203` (nil happenedAt falls back to defaults 'now').
- **Why it matters (iOS-side):** Android's shared serializer emits `value.toString()`, which omits the seconds field when seconds and nanos are zero (e.g. `2026-08-26T14:23+02:00`), and every picker-set date is built with seconds zero. iOS's `ISO8601Flexible` accepts only fractional or whole-second forms, so an Android-written draft resumed on iOS decodes `happened_at` to nil and hydration silently falls back to 'now', breaking the drafts design's 'restored verbatim' rule and corrupting the recorded moment. Postgres and web tolerate the form, so the break is invisible everywhere except the Android to iOS resume path, and iOS has no fixture covering Android's real emissions.
- **Fix:** Give `OffsetDateTimeSerializer.serialize` a deliberate emission (truncate to whole seconds and format with seconds always present, mirroring iOS `ISO8601Flexible.string`). Add the seconds-less and nanosecond variants as decode fixtures to iOS `DraftCrossSurfaceDecodingTests` and to the Android encode tests as a pinned-output assertion (not a same-surface round-trip).

#### 🟡 F-2026-08-TST-cross-surface-02 — The drafts contract's proof machinery never grew an Android-shaped payload: harness and iOS foreign-payload tests cover only web and iOS shapes

- **Criterion:** TST-02 (Shared shapes tested against real cross-surface payloads)
- **Priority:** P1 · **Cost:** S · **Impact × Likelihood:** 2 × 5 = 10 (Medium)
- **Where:** `packages/supabase/scripts/verify-pebble-drafts.ts:186-240` (iosShaped + webShaped only; no Android shape) and `:28-29` (standing rule scoped to keys, not surfaces); `apps/ios/PebblesTests/DraftCrossSurfaceDecodingTests.swift:12-32` (three fixtures, none Android-shaped); `apps/android/app/src/main/kotlin/app/pbbls/android/services/PebbleDraftsService.kt:74-81` (Android is a real third producer); `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/Models/PebbleDraftPayload.swift:61-79` (encodeIfPresent, iOS drafts omit nulls, contradicting the fixture labeled iOS-shaped).
- **Why it matters (iOS-side):** The committed drafts proof covers an iosShaped payload (whole seconds, explicit nulls) and a webShaped payload (milliseconds); no androidShaped fixture exists, although Android became the third writer of the same contract. iOS's `DraftCrossSurfaceDecodingTests` covers web-milliseconds, Postgres-microseconds, and whole-seconds, never Android's actual emissions, which is exactly how the incompatibility in the previous finding shipped unseen. The iosShaped fixture has also drifted: it carries explicit nulls, which the iOS draft encoder (encodeIfPresent throughout) never writes, so it is the create-payload shape, not what iOS drafts actually produce.
- **Fix:** Add an androidShaped fixture to `verify-pebble-drafts.ts` capturing Android's real emissions verbatim (minute-precision seconds-less timestamp, non-UTC offset, sub-second variant), add the same strings to iOS `DraftCrossSurfaceDecodingTests` and Android `DraftCrossSurfaceDecodingTest`, and extend the harness's standing-rule comment to trigger on new producing surfaces as well as new keys.

#### 🟡 F-2026-08-REL-cross-surface-01 — The M51 privacy-grade reinterpretation backfilled pebbles but not pebble_drafts, so pre-M51 drafts publish as connections-visible without a fresh owner choice

- **Criterion:** REL-06 (Contract-safe migrations with rollback story)
- **Priority:** P1 · **Cost:** S · **Impact × Likelihood:** 4 × 2 = 8 (Medium)
- **Where:** `packages/supabase/supabase/migrations/20260817130000_pebble_visibility_grades.sql:30-36` (backfill updates `public.pebbles` only; no `pebble_drafts` statement); `docs/decisions/log.md:386-389` (M51 entry); `apps/web/components/record/draft-payload.ts:44-46` and `155`; `apps/ios/Pebbles/Features/Path/Models/PebbleDraftPayload.swift:207-209` (`Visibility(rawValue:)` keeps `.private`); `apps/android/.../features/path/models/PebbleDraftPayload.kt:168`; `apps/android/.../features/path/record/RecordFlowModel.kt:238`.
- **Why it matters (iOS-side):** M51 reinterpreted visibility 'private' from owner-only to connections-visible and backfilled every `pebbles` row to 'secret' to prevent a silent privacy regression, but touched only `public.pebbles`. All three clients always write visibility into `pebble_drafts.payload`, and between drafts shipping and grade activation every draft was saved with the then-default 'private' (owner-only). No sweep or hydration mapping exists for drafts: iOS `PebbleDraft(payload:known:)` restores 'private' verbatim and publishing passes it straight to `create_pebble`, producing a pebble visible to every mutual connection. The record flow lands a resumed draft on the privacy step pre-selected, but a user who remembers choosing 'private' as owner-only has no cue that its meaning changed.
- **Fix:** Ship one migration sweeping stale drafts: `update pebble_drafts set payload = jsonb_set(payload, '{visibility}', '"secret"') where updated_at < '2026-08-17' and payload->>'visibility' = 'private'` (same updated_at-trigger caution as the pebbles backfill). Optionally add the same guard for web localStorage autosave snapshots at hydration.
