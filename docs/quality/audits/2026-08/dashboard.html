Method. 27 auditor cells (surface × domain cluster) plus a cross-surface contract auditor and a repo-wide agentic-readiness pass, each scoring every applicable criterion 0 to 4 against observable anchors with file:line evidence, then emitting findings. Every Critical and High finding went through an independent adversarial refutation attempt before retention. Risk = impact × likelihood; priority = severity × remediation cost.
