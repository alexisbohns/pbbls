Date: 2026-10-29. Decided by the maintainer through two blind games.

## How the set was chosen
1. Round 1 (h01–h10): ten hands from a DNA paragraph that listed example subjects; all converged on one vocabulary.
2. Round 2 (h11–h20): five free hands (all chose knotted cords) and five seeded from random strings (diverged).
3. Blind pick-three game over all twenty hands, three contexts, 69 steps, unlimited specials: the icon-points leader per icon became the composite (h00).
4. Round 3 (h21–h23): three hands redrew the composite in the Riverbed Reed line (h03).
5. Blind per-icon picker between the three round-3 variants: the final set (h99).

### Carved glyph stones (2026-10-29)

`pebble-dotted-glyph`, `pebble-dotted-glyph-2`, `pebble-outlined-glyph` and `pebble-outlined-glyph-2` light up as a solid stone with the glyph **carved out** of it, not as a plain silhouette: one compound path, `fill-rule="evenodd"`, the stone outline plus the glyph stroke buffered at the 1.75 stroke width with round caps (`carve.py`, shapely). The knockout shows the surface colour through the stone. Both iOS asset catalogs and Android vector drawables (`fillType="evenOdd"`) carry this. `pebble-round` and `pebble-flat` get the same treatment with their shine stroke carved out. Only people stays a plain silhouette.
