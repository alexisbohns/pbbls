# Ember Reed

- home: a tent with crossed poles and an open door; active: a small fire in the doorway.
- path: a winding walk climbing to a stone at its end; active: that stone is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one half behind the other; active: both head-stones lit.
- collections: three stones gathered in an open bowl; active: the top stone lit.
- user: one faceless figure, head-stone and shoulders; active: the head-stone lit.
- like: a heart scratched in two strokes that cross at the point; active: an ember inside it.
- Modes: stack = a toppled heap with one stone rolled off; pack = a tied pouch; track = three stones strung on a cord.

The hand owns stones as its only round shape and embers as its only fill: every circle is a pebble, and anything that comes alive is a stone or flame catching light.
