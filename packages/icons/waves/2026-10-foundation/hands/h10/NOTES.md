# Lifted Pen (h10)

- home: a tent with an open door; active: a small fire burning in the doorway.
- path: stepping stones strung along a winding trail; active: the latest stone is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one half behind the other; active: a lit stone in the front chest.
- collections: stones resting in a bowl; active: the top stone is lit.
- user: one faceless figure; active: a lit stone in the chest.
- like: a petroglyph handprint; active: a lit stone in the palm.

The hand owns the lifted pen: every stone and ring stays open where the pen left the paper, and only a lit stone ever closes.
