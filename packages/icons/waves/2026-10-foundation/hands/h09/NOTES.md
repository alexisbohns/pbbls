# Cairn Ink (h09)

- home: a tipi of two crossed poles over the ground, door open; active: a fire burns in the doorway.
- path: a winding walk with month-stones beside it; active: the stones are lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one a step behind; active: their heads are lit stones.
- collections: a bowl holding three stones; active: the stones are lit.
- user: one faceless figure; active: the head is a lit stone.
- like: a heart-shaped pebble with a highlight; active: lit from inside, a kernel glowing within the outline.
- Modes: stack is a tumbled heap, pack a bundle tied to a stick, track stones climbing one after another; settings are knotted cords, karma a petroglyph spiral, idea a sprouting seed, success a quiet sunrise.

The hand owns the stone: every ring, head, node and lens is the same lopsided pebble that never quite closes, and every active state lights a stone rather than filling a shape.
