# Slate Scratch (h04)

- home: a tipi with crossed poles and an arched door; active: a fire lit in the doorway.
- path: three stepping stones climbing in a zigzag; active: the stone you stand on now is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one behind the other; active: both head-stones lit.
- collections: a bowl heaped with three stones; active: the top stone lit.
- user: one faceless figure, head-stone and shoulders; active: the head-stone lit.
- like: a heart in two strokes that almost meet; active: a lit stone at its centre.
- modes: stack = a loose pile of four stones; pack = a tied sack; track = three stones in a row on a line.

The hand owns the stone: every node, head, lens and knob is the same lopsided pebble, and coming alive always means that stone lights.
