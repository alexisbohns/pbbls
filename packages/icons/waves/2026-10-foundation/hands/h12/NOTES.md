# Cord Keeper (h12)

- World: the knot-cord records of a river people, who tie one knot for every day worth keeping and press the cord into wet clay to leave its mark.
- Active event: a knot is pulled tight; the loose loop cinches into one small dark bead.
- Line rule: every stroke is a cord laid down, so it sags between its ends and never stops dead; its tail hooks or curls.

Tabs and their active twins:
- home: a cord coiled at rest on the ground / the knot tied at the heart of the coil
- path: the month cord rising, a loop thrown for each month / today's knot cinched at its tip
- people: three strands bound in one knot, fanning out / the binding knot pulled tight
- collections: a drawstring pouch, its neck loosely tied / the neck knot cinched
- user: your own cord worn round the neck with one ring-stone / the stone's knot lit inside it
- like: two cords hooked into each other / the clasp cinched into a bead
