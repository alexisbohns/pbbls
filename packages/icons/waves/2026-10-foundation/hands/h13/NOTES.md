# Knot Keeper (h13)

World: Andean khipu keepers: dyed cords hung from a main cord, memory tied in knots and read with the fingers.
Active event: a loose knot is pulled tight; the open wrap-tick becomes one small solid bead.
Line rule: every stroke is a cord, so it never ends sharp; it trails, hooks or crosses itself where a knot sits.

- home: a rope coil lying at rest | active: a bead pulled tight in the coil's eye
- path: one rising cord with a knot-tick per month, its end curling loose | active: the newest tick pulled into a bead
- people: three strands from one tie, each looped into a head | active: the shared tie becomes a bead
- collections: a khipu main cord with three pendants | active: the middle pendant's knot pulled tight
- user: one strand looping into a head, with a wrap below | active: the wrap becomes a bead
- like: a keepsake loop whose ends cross at the top | active: the crossing tied into a bead
