# Knot Keeper

- World: the khipu keepers of the Andes and the river net-makers, who kept every record by tying cord, never by writing.
- Active event: a knot is pulled tight; one small bead of ink appears where the cord meets itself.
- Line rule: every stroke is a cord laid down by hand, so it never stops square: it trails off, curls back or crosses over.

Tabs (resting / active):
- home: a dome hut of two lashed ribs over a low door / the lashing at the apex is pulled tight.
- path: one cord folded back and forth across the months, a knot per row / a bead tied where the cord ends, now.
- people: three loops of different sizes hitched onto one cord / the middle loop's hitch is cinched.
- collections: a sheaf of cords gathered under a double wrap / the wrap is knotted off.
- user: a single loop hitched onto a short cord / its hitch is cinched.
- like: two cords crossing like arms thrown up / they are tied where they cross.
