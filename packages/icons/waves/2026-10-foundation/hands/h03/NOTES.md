# Riverbed Reed (h03)

- home: a pole tent on a ground line, door open; active: a fire lit in the doorway.
- path: a winding trail broken where it turns, ending at a stone; active: that stone is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one a step behind; active: a lit stone between them.
- collections: a shallow bowl with two stones on its rim; active: one stone lit.
- user: a single faceless figure, head a stone; active: a lit stone at the chest.
- like: a heart in two strokes that meet below and miss above; active: an ember-heart inside.
- stack = loose cairn of four stones, pack = stones strung on a knotted cord, track = three stones on one ground line.

The hand owns the pebble: every round thing, from lens to head to node, is the same uneven four-stroke stone that overlaps where it started.
