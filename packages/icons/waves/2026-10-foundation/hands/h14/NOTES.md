# Knot Cord (h14)

- World: the knotted record-cords of herders who kept their year on string; every mark is a length of plied cord, laid, hung, wound or tied.
- Active event: a knot is pulled tight on the cord, and that knot is lit.
- Line rule: every stroke is one cord with two loose ends; it sags under its own weight and crosses other cords instead of joining them.

- home: a doorway hung from a sagging lintel cord, a pull-string in the middle; active: the pull-string is knotted.
- path: a ball of wound thread with its free end trailing out to now; active: the free end is knotted.
- people: two strands plied around each other; active: a knot binds them where they cross.
- collections: a net bag gathered at the neck by a drawstring; active: the drawstring is knotted shut.
- user: an amulet stone hung on a cord around the neck; active: a knot where the cord takes the stone.
- like: a loose overhand loop; active: the loop is pulled tight into a knot.
