# Reed Scratch (h07)

- home: a tent of two crossed poles over a ground line, door open; active: a fire lit in the doorway.
- path: a winding trail that leads up to one stone on the horizon; active: that stone is lit (you are here).
- people: two faceless figures, stone heads, one behind the other; active: both heads lit.
- collections: a bowl with two stones resting on its rim; active: the larger stone is lit.
- user: one faceless figure, stone head over a shoulder arch; active: the head is lit.
- like: a heart drawn as two strokes that almost meet; active: a small warm heart lit inside.
- Others: stone ring for check/plus, sand surface in the glass, stone lens, tilted heap (stack), tied sack (pack), stones in a row over an arrow (track), stone-handled sliders (settings), petroglyph spiral (karma), stone radiating three rays (success), spark (idea).

The hand owns the stone: every node, handle, head and lens is the same small irregular pebble, and lighting one is how anything comes alive.
