# River Slate (h01)

Tab icons and their active twins:
- home: a two-pole tent with a door flap; active: a fire burns in the doorway.
- path: a meandering walk ending at a stone; active: the stone at the end is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one behind the other; active: a stone glows in the near figure's chest.
- collections: two stones resting on a bowl; active: the upper stone is lit.
- user: one faceless figure; active: a stone glows in its chest.
- like: a lumpy, uneven heart; active: a lit stone at its centre.

The hand owns the small stone: every ring, head, node and lit thing is the same leaning pebble, so the set reads as one bag of stones.
