# Bark Reed

Tab subjects and their active twins:
- home: a tent with an open doorway; active, a fire burns in the doorway.
- path: a winding trail ending at a stone; active, the stone at the end is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, one behind the other; active, both heads are lit stones.
- collections: a bowl holding two stones; active, the stones in the bowl are lit.
- user: one faceless figure; active, its head is a lit stone.
- like: a heart whose two strokes don't quite meet at the bottom; active, a lit stone at its core.

The hand owns the near-miss: strokes overshoot or stop just short, and nothing quite closes, so the paper always gets in.
