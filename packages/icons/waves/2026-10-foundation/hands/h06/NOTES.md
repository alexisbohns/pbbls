# Cairn Reed

Tab icons and their active twins:
- home: a tent of two crossed poles over a ground line, door open; active = a flame lit in the doorway.
- path: a switchback trail climbing to a stone; active = the stone at the top is lit.
- people: two faceless figures, the second half-hidden; active = a lit heart in each.
- collections: a shallow bowl holding three stones; active = the middle stone lit.
- user: one faceless figure, head a stone; active = the heart lit.
- like: a heart of two strokes that almost meet at the tip; active = a stone lit inside it.

The hand owns the stone: every ring, head, node and seed is the same lopsided pebble, so the set reads as one pile.
