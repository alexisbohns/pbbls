# h08 — Creek Reed

- home: a hide tent on the ground line, door flap closed. Active: the flaps tied open and a small fire lit inside.
- path: a winding trail climbing the canvas with three stones beside its bends. Active: the three stones lit, the months you walked.
- people: two faceless figures, stone heads, one half behind the other. Active: both heads lit.
- collections: a shallow bowl holding three stones, one heaped on top. Active: the top stone lit.
- user: one faceless figure, stone head and shoulders. Active: the head lit.
- like: an uneven heart drawn in one stroke. Active: a small lit stone set inside it.
- Modes: stack is a loose heap with one stone rolled away, pack a drawstring pouch, track three stones in a row on one ground line.

The hand owns the stone: every head, node, slider and bead is the same lopsided pebble, and "active" always means that stone has been lit.
