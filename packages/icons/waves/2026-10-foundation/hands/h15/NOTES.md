# Cord Keeper (h15)

- World: the Andean khipu — memory kept as knots tied in spun cord, hung from a main cord and read by touch.
- Active event: a knot is tied; a small bead of cord tightens onto the line (the one lit fill).
- Line rule: every stroke is a cord: it sags under its own weight, never runs dead straight, and its ends hang loose.

Tab icons and their active twins:
- home: a cord coiled at rest on the floor; active, the coil's inner end is knotted.
- path: a main cord with three pendants of different lengths, the months; active, a knot on the middle pendant.
- people: two cords from opposite sides hooked into each other; active, a knot binds the clasp.
- collections: a tassel, strands gathered under one wrap and hanging loop; active, a knot on the middle strand.
- user: one cord with an eye splice and its whipping; active, a knot tied below the whipping.
- like: a cord slung between two hooked ends; active, a knot hangs at the bottom of the sag.
