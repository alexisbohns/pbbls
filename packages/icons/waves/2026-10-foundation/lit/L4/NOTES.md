# L4 — "the solid hand" (64 variants)
Each light is the stone itself made solid: one unstroked path that follows the stone's own curves, closed where the stroke overshoots or stops short, and inset by 0.3. Where another stroke crosses a solid stone (a fold, strap, glyph, highlight, or a neighbour's edge), the light carries a counter-wound cut-out 0.7 wider than that stroke, so the line stays readable on the solid. The two dotted pebbles are filled out to the dashes' outer edge (outset 0.85), so they read as one smooth solid stone and not as a cog.
- path: path (bottom → middle → top) and path-river (lower right → left → upper right, walking up the river). destination and yarn have one stone each.
- pouches: the body is the one stone (lit-1). Folds, straps, the purse flap and clasp, and the baluchon stick are cut out.
- stones: stair and track go bottom-left upward. arrow-right and aligned-bottom go left → right.
- stacks: stack and stack-sharp go front-left, back-right, then the top stone.
- pebbles: one stone each. The round and flat pebbles keep their glint and the dotted pebbles keep their glyph, cut out.
- calendar: calendar-day lights its day ring only.
- plants: the seed, the lower leaf, then the upper leaf. plant-sprout has no seed, so it lights the lower leaf, then the upper leaf.
- knots: the left loop, then the right loop.
- users: the bodies first and a head last. user and user-heart light the body, then the head. users lights the front body, the back body (cut clear of the front body), then the front head. people lights the left body, the right body, then the left head. person lights only its spiral head.
Fewer than three: 1 stone for destination, yarn, the seven pouches, the seven pebbles, calendar-day and person. 2 stones for plant-sprout, knot-sm, knot-lg, user and user-heart.
Skipped: abstract-stack (open arcs and a dot), abstract-cairn (one open spiral, so a solid fill reads as a smear), fall (curls), calendar-date (a numeral), calendar-dot (a curl). knot-lg's centre knot is too small: filling it changes almost nothing.
