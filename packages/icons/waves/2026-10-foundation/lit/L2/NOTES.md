# L2 — lit series (63 variants)

Method: each light is an unstroked filled blob (`fill="currentColor" stroke="none"`) sized so its edge sits at least 0.35 units clear of every stroke's inked edge. With the 1.75 stroke, a stroked blob could not fit inside the small stones without touching their outline. One exception: `person` reuses the foundation's own light for that drawing (`user-active`) verbatim, because its spiral head has no clear room at all.

Lighting order per family:
- path: in walking order, nearest or lowest stone first (path and path-river: bottom, middle, top). destination and yarn: lit-1 only (one stone, one ball).
- pouches: the body is the one round, so lit-1 only (pouch, pouch-2, pouch-dot, purse, baluchon, bag, case). Purse lights to the left of the clasp, not under it, so it doesn't read as "!". Case lights the middle compartment.
- stones: lowest first (stair, track). Rows go left to right, following the arrow and the reading direction (arrow-right, aligned-bottom).
- stacks: bottom up, front stone before back (stack, stack-sharp, abstract-cairn: base, middle curl, under the cap). abstract-stack: the two bands, bottom then top, so 2 variants; its top dot is already solid.
- pebbles: one stone each, so lit-1 only. The light sits below and right of centre, away from the highlight. On the dotted pebbles it sits off the glyph's axis, so it doesn't read as "?".
- calendar: calendar-day lights its day circle (lit-1). The rings are single strokes with no inside, so they are not lit.
- plants: seed first (plant-seedling, plant-seedling-gap: lit-1). Their leaves leave less than 1 unit of clear space inside, so the leaves are not lit.
- knots: left loop, then right loop (2 each). knot-lg's centre is smaller than the stroke.
- users: chest first, head last. user and user-heart: 2. users and people: front or left chest, back or right chest, then the front or left head (3 of their 4 rounds).

Fewer than three stones: destination, yarn, every pouch, every pebble, calendar-day, plant-seedling, plant-seedling-gap and person (1 each); abstract-stack, the knots, user and user-heart (2 each).
No variant: fall, plant-sprout, calendar-date and calendar-dot. Their stones are narrower than the stroke (fall's curls, sprout's leaves), or the drawing has no round with an inside (the numeral, the day dot).
