# L1 — "the measured hand" (64 variants)
Lights are unstroked filled blobs (`stroke="none"`). Each one is placed by clearance search at about 60% of the stone's free interior, with a gap of 0.3 or more to the outline's inner edge, and the light shapes are capped at a 2.6 semi-axis. The one exception is person's head, which reuses user-active's foundation blob verbatim.
- path: path, bottom → middle → top. path-river, in walking order up the river: lower right, then left, then upper right. destination and yarn have one round each.
- pouches (all seven): the body is the only round, so each gets `-lit-1` only. The light sits in the largest clear area of the body, away from the folds and the clasp.
- stones: stair and track go bottom/left first, arrow-right goes left → right, aligned-bottom goes left → right.
- stacks: stack and stack-sharp go bottom-left, then bottom-right, then the top stone. abstract-cairn goes bottom stone, then middle loop, then under the cap arch. abstract-stack lights the two bands under its arcs, bottom up (the dot is too small).
- pebbles: one stone each (`-lit-1`), lit off the highlight arc and the glyph.
- calendar: calendar-day lights its day mark only, because the rings are straight lines with no inside.
- plants: seedling and seedling-gap light the seed only, because the leaves are too thin for a gapped light.
- knots: knot-sm and knot-lg go left loop, then right loop. knot-lg's centre knot is too small.
- users: user, user-heart and person light the body first (for person, the bowl of the arms) and the head last. users lights the front torso, then the back torso, then the front head. people lights the left torso, then the right torso, then the left head.
Fewer than three: destination, yarn, the seven pouches, the seven pebbles, calendar-day, plant-seedling and plant-seedling-gap have 1. abstract-stack, knot-sm, knot-lg, person, user and user-heart have 2.
No variant (no interior wide enough to hold a light): fall (the curls are smaller than the stroke), calendar-date and calendar-dot (no round: the rings are lines, the day mark is a numeral or a dot), and plant-sprout (no seed, leaves too thin).
