# L3 — the lit series
Lights are small filled ellipses centred on each stone's widest clear spot, kept at least 0.4 (0.28 on the smallest stair stone) off every stroke; the outline always stays visible. Where a stone is too tight for a stroked light, the drawing's lights carry `stroke="none"` (the visible size is the same). Person's head reuses the foundation `user-active` light verbatim.
Lighting order per family:
- path: in walking order, from the lowest stone to the top (path, path-river: bottom right, then left, then top right).
- stones: stair and track from bottom-left upward; arrow-right and aligned-bottom from left to right, the way the arrow reads.
- stacks: bottom up (stack and stack-sharp: front-left, then right, then top; abstract-cairn: base, middle, cap).
- pebbles, pouches: one light low in the body.
- calendar: the day mark only (calendar-day's ring).
- plants: the seed first.
- knots: left loop, then right loop.
- users: bodies first, the head last (users: front body, back body, front head; people: left body, right body, left head).
Fewer than three stones (-lit-1 only): destination, yarn, pouch, pouch-2, pouch-dot, purse, baluchon, bag, case, pebble-round, pebble-flat, pebble-glow-sm/-md/-lg, pebble-dotted-glyph(-2), calendar-day, plant-seedling, plant-seedling-gap.
-lit-1 and -lit-2 only: abstract-stack (the top dot is too small to hold a light), knot-sm, knot-lg (the centre knot is too tight), person (arms, then head), user, user-heart.
No variants: fall (the three falling stones are curls about 1.1 across, with no room for a light), calendar-date (the "1" and the rings are strokes), calendar-dot (the dot is already solid ink), plant-sprout (no seed; the leaves on every plant are too thin to light).
Sources pebble-dotted-glyph(-2) carry stroke-dasharray in their copied outlines. They are kept verbatim; the lights have no dasharray.
