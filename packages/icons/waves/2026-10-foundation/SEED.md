## Seed (read before the brief)
Your hand is grown from this random seed: `{SEED}`

Do this first, in your NOTES, and show your work in three lines:
1. Read the seed as a string of accidents. Pull out of it: a material (from the letters), a place or people (from the digits), a gesture of the hand (from the punctuation and case). Be literal and strange; do not pick what you would have picked anyway.
2. Turn those three into the world your hand draws from, the event that makes an icon active, and the rule of your line.
3. Only then draw. Every icon comes from that world. If the seed gives you something awkward, keep it; the awkwardness is the point.
