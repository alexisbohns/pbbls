# The community wave (2026-10-31)

One figure, drawn for the Lab section of the You tab (Type Bench exploration, wave 2): the callout that invites people to the Pebbles WhatsApp community.

- `whatsapp`: the WhatsApp mark redrawn in the Riverbed Reed line (DNA.md). The bubble is the set's uneven stone, closed by overlap where the stroke began, with its tail leaning out at the lower left; the handset is one stroke with its two pads, no fill. No twin: it is a destination, nothing lights in it.

Brand note: Meta's WhatsApp brand guidelines ask that the logo not be altered. This drawing is an adaptation in the house style; check the guidelines before it ships in a store build, and fall back to the official glyph if they forbid it.
