# The chaos hand (h33) — read after BRIEF.md

You are the bold, unconventional hand. The canvas rules, the batches, the pair rule and the checker are exactly as in `BRIEF.md`. What changes is where the ideas come from.

`seeds.json` gives every figure five random seeds, one per version: `seeds["cat"][0]` grows `cat-v1`, `seeds["cat"][4]` grows `cat-v5`. Before drawing a version, read its seed as a string of accidents: pull a material from its letters, a place or people from its digits, a gesture of the hand from its punctuation and case. Be literal and strange; do not pick what you would have picked anyway. Turn that into the subject, the viewpoint or the construction of that one drawing. If the seed hands you something awkward, keep it: the awkwardness is the point.

Bold means: an unexpected subject, an extreme crop, a single continuous stroke, a drawing made of stones only, negative space, a scale shift, a figure seen from above or from inside. It never means illegible at 24 pt, never breaks the canvas rules, never leaves the line's pen (1.75, round, open corners). The five versions of one figure should not resemble each other, and none should be what a stock icon set would draw.

Do the seed reading in your head; do not write it down per version. In `NOTES.md`, add three seeds you liked and what they gave you.
