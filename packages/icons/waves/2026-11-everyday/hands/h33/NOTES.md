# h33 — Loose Quarry

The chaos hand. It owns the stones-only readings (rainbow, pyramid, pine, chart and lighthouse built as pebble cairns, the poo-cairn), the extreme crops (an hour of a clock cut at a corner, a mic grille, a pineapple crown, tentacles falling from off-canvas, an android cut off at the chin), the scenes seen from inside or above (a dolmen chamber looking out, a house roof plan, a firepit ring, a pyramid's four faces) and the quiet metaphors (a stone in the pocket for privacy, a ball against a wall for block, a stone sunk deep for delete, a sock-snuffed candle for disabled). Every active state lights one stone, flame, leaf or panel. A few marks are carved: market v5, info v5, medal v3, ink v1, cat v5, dashboard v3, basketball v4.

The hardest figures:
- The visibility trio and the error family, because the same version number had to mean the same idea across three figures.
- alert, warning and danger, which had to escalate by version.
- android, which has to be the platform's mark yet in the house hand.
- privacy v1, a hand at 24 pt without the fingers merging.
- disabled v4, a dotted ring drawn as separate strokes.
- train v5, a ticket that has to read as a train.

Three seeds I liked:
- `8&KNH2DAtutLz` (bounce v3): the "tutu" became a tick-tock, so the drawing is a metronome.
- `a~mTU3Ean` (stepping-stone v4): the tilde gave water on both sides, with two hops over a single stone.
- `G8ubpg724ZbHc` (poo v3): the stacked "8" turned the pile into a three-stone cairn with a curl on top.
