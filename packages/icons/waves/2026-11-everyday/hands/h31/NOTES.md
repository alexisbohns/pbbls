# h31: Quiet Mason

What this hand owns: the house stone used as a working part, not as decoration. Across most figures v1 is the plain reading, v2 says it with stones only, and v3 to v5 change the object or the metaphor. Families keep one vocabulary per version number:
- error set: v2 stones, v3 tears, v4 empty places, v5 something that got away (empty hook, snapped bead, paper plane)
- visibility trio: v2 stone ring, then satellites, then ripples; v3 closed door, then ajar, then archway; v4 closed eye, then half-lidded, then open; v5 buried, then nested, then on the summit
- appearance trio: v3 window panes, v4 horizon (sun, moon, setting sun), v5 open flower, closed bud, half-lit daisy
- alert, warning, danger: v3 a crack that spreads, v4 a gauge needle that climbs, v5 cloud, then storm, then funnel
- minus, remove, delete: v4 a water level falls, a stone is lifted out, a stone sinks
- rock, boulder, stepping-stone: v2 pebble facet, then a giant beside a pebble, then stones in a row over water

Hardest: privacy and secret (keeping them apart from locked and from the hand), info (it has to work at caption size and still read apart from alert), android (the robot head redrawn without becoming a copy), mute v3 (zipped mouth), crab v3 (hermit spiral), bounce, and life-domain.

Figures I'd look at again: crab-v5 (its eyes sit under the ground line), store-v1 (one awning scallop doubles back), dashboard-v4 and trunk-v4 (in the active twin the stroke paths are split differently, though they draw the same lines), and the body fills that run past 64 units: cloud-v1, umbrella-v1, rock-v1, poo-v1, tree-v1/v2.
