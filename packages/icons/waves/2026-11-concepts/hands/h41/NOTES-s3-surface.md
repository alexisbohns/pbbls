- expand / collapse: the classical corner arrows are line-only, so their solids are skipped; the centre dot as a tail mark is the only honest mark.
- disabled: hatching is the clearest "unavailable" sign but has no place for a mark (marked skipped); the cracked stone with a fallen chip carries the pebbles reading.
- inform: the expected info sign is a letter, so the classical is a circle with a stem and the marked adds the dot; the pebbles idea is a waystone.
- matrix weird (a weave) has no closed body: solid skipped, tail dot on a strand.
- affordance pebbles is a worry stone with a thumb dip: the one concept where the stone world gave the most direct answer.
