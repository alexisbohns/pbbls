# h41 · s6-trust notes

- gdpr: hardest. A legal framework has no object of its own, and the flag and ring of stars are banned, so every idea leans on a stand-in (a sealed document, scales, a carved tablet, a rosette seal).
- data-protection: it overlaps privacy and security. The fingerprint under a sheltering arc was the only reading that was clearly about personal data.
- store-compliance: "allowed in" is abstract. I used a door with an entering arrow and a puzzle piece that fits, but neither says "store" without context.
- inclusion: the solids fused easily (heads, a ring of stones). Every repeated shape had to be kept at least 2 units apart.
- anxiety and avoid-hassles: the weird ideas are single open lines (a scribble, a knot pulled loose), so their solids are skipped.
- doctor: with no face and no cross, only the stethoscope, the bag and the head mirror read as a doctor.
