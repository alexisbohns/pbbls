# h41 · s1-delivery notes

- log vs record vs ledger vs journal vs history: five neighbours that all want "a page with lines". I split them by gesture: append (receipt tape, tally, threaded stones), one entry (index card, framed scribble), columns (T-account, stacked stone columns), day after day (a row of daily stones), looking back (road to the horizon, a cairn, growth rings).
- release: the rocket is the expected sign, but "a numbered version" has no numerals to lean on. The ticks on the tag and the stones growing in size have to carry the numbering.
- beta: no Greek letter allowed, so "early" became a sprout, the first circle of heads, and an unfinished ring.
- commitment: a handshake is too detailed at 24 units. The seal on a signed sheet and two linked rings say "pledge" more cleanly.
- Solid variants of line-only ideas (flow, changelog weird, ledger weird) needed a body invented for them: a filled ribbon, column slabs and heavier dots. They are the weakest of the solids.
