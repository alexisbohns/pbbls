# h41 · s8-meaning

- transcendence: "going beyond" has no object; I leaned on direction (an arrow through a ceiling, a line escaping a circle) and the floating stone over a cairn.
- self-actualization: the Maslow pyramid is the only stock reading; the flower and the door at the top of the stairs carry it less directly. The flower's petals had to move off the centre so the solid would not fuse into one blob.
- nostalgia: hard to draw without a clock or photo cliché; the warm stone with rising wisps is the closest to "a memory warmed by time".
- community-weird (Venn rings) has no honest solid: filling three overlapping rings makes a clover blob, so it is skipped.
- belonging and legacy both drift towards cairns in the pebbles idea; I kept them apart (a nestled stone vs a cairn left beside a trail).
