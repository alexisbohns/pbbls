# h41 · s5-status notes

Vocabularies: classical = a stone-circle chip with the sign inside (bars and a "!" box for priorities); bold = a flag on a pole (its height and shape tell the status) and a gauge needle for priorities; pebbles = a stone in its situation (row, cairn, pouch, river, strata) and cairn height for priorities; weird = a plant's life (seed, sprout, bud, flower, wilt) and the sun's height over a horizon for priorities.

- status-superseded / status-supersede: hardest to read as a pair. In every idea "this one" is the big shape, and the arrow, flag height or stratum always runs old to new. Superseded puts the big shape on the old side, supersede puts it on the new side.
- status-draft: an unfinished state still needs a closed body for the solid. I used a pencil, a scribble, a rough faceted stone and a half-drawn vein.
- priority-undefined: "not ranked" has no natural image. I used dashes, an empty gauge hub, a stone hovering above the ground and a sun behind a cloud. The pebbles one is the weakest.
- status-rejected (weird): a plant hanging upside down ("turned down") needs a second look.
- Dots carry the stroke too, so their visible size is about 4. Tail dots near circle edges had to be placed by hand to keep clear of the outline.
