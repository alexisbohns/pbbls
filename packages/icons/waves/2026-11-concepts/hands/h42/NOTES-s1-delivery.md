- commitment: a promise has no object. I tried a seal with ribbons, hooked arms, a stone bound with cord, and a loop that comes back to where it started. The hooked arms are the weakest.
- beta vs test: both pull toward lab glass. I kept the flask for beta, so test became a clipboard check, a probe dipped in a cup, a stone tested on water, and a scribble.
- log vs journal vs history vs record: all four are a list of something. I set them apart by metaphor: knots on a cord, a pebble a day, strata, one carved stone.
- solid variants of line-only ideas (flow waves, tally, barcodes, diff signs) have no body to fill, so their fills thicken bars or bands rather than fill a silhouette.
