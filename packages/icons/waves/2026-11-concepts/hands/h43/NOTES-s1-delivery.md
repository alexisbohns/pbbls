# h43 notes, s1-delivery

- commitment: the hardest abstract idea; no obvious object, so ring / linked rings / tied stone / threaded holey stone all lean on metaphor.
- flow and backlog: motion and waiting have no body to fill, so the solids rely on a node, a lobe or a few stones.
- log and ledger: both near the same ruled-page family as changelog and record, so the ideas had to branch (receipt, tally stick, tube of stones, balance).
- decision: a fork needs two directions; the pebbles and weird ideas show choosing (lifted stone, split stone) rather than a Y.
- solid carves (ledger table cells, changelog diff card) are fine-grained and worth a look at small sizes.
