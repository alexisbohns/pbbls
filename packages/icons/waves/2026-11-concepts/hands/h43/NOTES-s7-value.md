# h43 notes, squad s7-value

Hardest concepts, and why:
- simplify, reduce-effort: no body of their own. "Less" and "easier" had to be told by a subtraction (a stripped page, a pruned stem, a lever, a shortcut line) rather than by an object.
- variety: a mix has no single silhouette. Kept to different closed shapes side by side (circle, triangle, square; four stones; three books) so solid still fills every kind.
- sensorial, aesthetics: five senses or beauty cannot fit one picture. Picked one sense each (eye, steam, hand, fingerprint) and one beautiful object (tulip, gem).
- badge-value, recognition: close to reward. Told apart by the seal (worth) and the lit stone / figure with rays (being seen).
- Marked dots that sit in small stones had to stay under 0.55 of the host (organize-pebbles was trimmed after a warning).
