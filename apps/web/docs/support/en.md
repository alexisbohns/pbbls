# Pebbles support

Pebbles is a calm place to keep what happened this week, who you shared it with, and how it felt.

## Contact us

Write to [ciao@pbbls.app](mailto:ciao@pbbls.app) with your question, or with what went wrong and on which device. There is no need to include anything you wrote in your pebbles.

## Delete your account

You can delete your account from Settings at any time. [How to delete your account](/docs/delete-account) gives the steps, what is erased, and what to do if you can't sign in.

## Your data and your rights

The [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy) explains what Pebbles keeps and why. The [Terms of Service](/docs/terms) and the [Legal Notice](/docs/legal-notice) cover the rest.
