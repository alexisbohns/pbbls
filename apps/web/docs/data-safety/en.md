# What Pebbles collects

This is the plain version of what we declare to Google Play in its Data safety form. The [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy) is the full version.

## In short

- We don't share your data with other companies, and we don't use it for advertising.
- Supabase stores it for us, on servers in Paris.
- It is encrypted on its way between your device and our servers.
- You can [delete some of your data](/docs/delete-data) or [your whole account](/docs/delete-account) at any time.

## What we collect

- **Your name**, shown in the app and to the people you connect with. Needed for your account. If you sign in with Google, it comes from your Google account.
- **Your email address**, to sign you in and send you emails about your account. Needed for your account.
- **Your account ID**, a random code that ties your data to you. Needed for your account.
- **Photos you add** to your pebbles. Optional.
- **How you feel**: the emotions, intensity, positiveness and life domains you record. This counts as health data. Needed to use the app.
- **What you write and make**: your moments and their names, your souls, collections and glyphs, and your reactions in the Lab. Needed to use the app.
- **Your activity**: your karma, your achievements and when you use the app. Needed to use the app.
- **Your onboarding answers**: why you came and how often you'd like to record. Optional. They choose the first prompt the app shows you.

## How we use it

- **To run the app:** everything above.
- **For your account:** your name, email address and account ID.
- **For statistics:** we count things like how many people use Pebbles each day, how many come back, and which emotions are recorded most. We count from our own database, and there are no analytics or advertising tools in the app.

## What we don't collect

- Your location
- Your phone's contacts
- Your emails, texts or calls
- Videos
- Voice or audio recordings
- Your files or calendar
- Payment or financial information
- Device or advertising identifiers
- Crash reports or diagnostics
- Your browsing history, searches or other apps
- Your address, phone number or other personal details
- Fitness data

## When this changes

If Pebbles starts collecting something new, this page and the Data safety form change in the same update, and the Privacy Policy says what changed.
