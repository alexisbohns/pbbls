# Delete your Pebbles account

You can delete your Pebbles account and everything in it at any time, without contacting anyone. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

To delete only some of your data and keep your account, see [Delete some of your data](/docs/delete-data).

## How to delete it

1. Sign in on the Pebbles app, or [on the web](/login).
2. Open the **You** tab, then **Settings**.
3. Scroll to the bottom and choose **Delete account**.
4. Confirm with **Delete forever**. You may be asked to confirm it's you first.

## If you can't sign in

Write to [ciao@pbbls.app](mailto:ciao@pbbls.app) from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will respond within one month.

## What is erased

These are erased immediately and permanently:

- Your account and profile
- Your pebbles and their photos
- Your souls and collections
- Your glyphs and karma

## What stays, and for how long

- **Glyphs other people bought** stay available to them, without your name attached.
- **Authentication logs**, the timestamps of your sign-ups, logins and logouts, are kept for 12 months for security, then deleted.
- **Backups** may hold deleted data for up to 90 days. After that, no trace remains.

The [Privacy Policy](/docs/privacy) sets out these periods in its section on data retention.
