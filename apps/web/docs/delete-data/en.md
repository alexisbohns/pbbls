# Delete some of your data

You can delete pebbles, souls and collections and keep your Pebbles account. There's no one to contact, and it can't be undone.

## Pebbles and moments

1. Open the **Path** tab in the app or [on the web](/login), then open the pebble.
2. Choose **Delete**.
3. Choose **Delete the pebble, keep the moment** to leave a blank stone on your path, or **Delete the moment too** to remove its photo as well.

## Souls

Linked pebbles stay. Only the soul and its links are removed.

1. Open the **People** tab.
2. On Android or iPhone, press and hold the soul, then choose **Delete**. On the web, open the soul and select the delete button.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

## Collections

Linked pebbles stay. Only the collection and its links are removed. You can do this in the Android and iPhone apps, but not yet on the web.

1. Open the **Collections** tab.
2. On Android, press and hold the collection, then choose **Delete**. On iPhone, swipe it to the left, then choose **Delete**.
3. Confirm with **Delete**.

## How long it takes

What you delete is erased immediately. [Backups](/docs/privacy) may hold it for up to 90 days. After that, no trace remains.

## Anything else

To have other data deleted, write to [ciao@pbbls.app](mailto:ciao@pbbls.app) from the email address on your account and tell us what to remove. We will respond within one month.

To delete everything, see [Delete your account](/docs/delete-account).
