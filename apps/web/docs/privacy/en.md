## Preamble

Pebbles is an application designed to help you collect and revisit your daily memories, in complete peace of mind. We believe that a good privacy policy is fundamentally an act of respect toward our users. This document explains, clearly and transparently, what data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what rights you have.

Pebbles functions as a personal and local application. We do not profile your emotions for advertising. We do not share your memories anywhere. Your data remains yours.

This privacy policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the guidelines of the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL).

## 2. Data Collected

### 2.1 Data Provided Directly by You

When you sign up and use Pebbles, you voluntarily provide us with the following data:

- **Account Data :** Your full name, email address, and avatar (profile photo).
- **Events :** Your free-text responses to structured questions (situation, emotions, thoughts, reactions, learnings, feedback) inspired by Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT).
- **Moods :** The type of mood recorded (daily mood or momentary emotion), your rating on a 7-point scale, up to 38 emotion labels, up to 18 associations with life domains, and intensity and impact scores.
- **Souls :** The first names or names of people you associate with your events (third-party data you enter yourself).

### 2.2 Data Generated by Your Usage

When you use Pebbles, we automatically generate:

- **Bounce Karma :** A metric indicating your regularity of engagement with the app.
- **Achievements and Progress :** The badges and milestones you unlock.
- **Cairns :** A weekly summary of your pebbles, computed by the app on your device each time it is displayed. It is never stored.

### 2.3 Technical Data

To operate and ensure your security, we process:

- **Authentication Logs :** The timestamps of your sign-ups, logins, and logouts (via Supabase audit logs).
- **Session Tokens :** The session cookies provided by Supabase to maintain your login.
- **Standard HTTP Headers :** Technical metadata about your device and browser.

### 2.4 Third-Party Processing

- **Fonts :** We use self-hosted fonts (Ysabeau OFL and system-ui). No external requests are made to font CDNs.

### 2.5 Third-Party Personal Data

If you record the names of people dear to you (your "souls"), these names are stored in your Pebbles account. Pebbles never contacts these people and never shares their data with them.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with Article 6 of the GDPR, we process your data on the following legal bases:

### 3.1 Contract Performance (Art. 6(1)(b))

Processing of the following data is necessary to perform the Pebbles service agreement:
- Account data (registration, identification)
- Events, moods, and responses (recording and storage)
- Bounce karma and achievements (core functionality)

### 3.2 Explicit Consent (Art. 9(2)(a))

The following data constitutes special category personal data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR and is processed only with your explicit consent:
- Moods and emotion labels (qualified as health data)
- CBT reflections and personal analysis
- Social sharing (if enabled)

You give the consent covering your moods, emotions and reflections when you create your account. If your account was created before we introduced that step, you can give it at any time from Settings. Publishing your public profile is a separate permission, which you give in Settings when you turn it on.

You may withdraw either at any time. Section 4.3 explains how, and what withdrawal means in practice.

### 3.3 Legitimate Interest (Art. 6(1)(f))

We use your data on the basis of our legitimate interests for:
- **Security :** Fraud prevention, authentication, and security maintenance (authentication logs).
- **Product Improvement :** Analysis of anonymized aggregates to enhance user experience.

### 3.4 Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

We process your data if required by law, including in response to judicial or administrative requests.

## 4. Special Category Data and Health Data

### 4.1 Qualification

The moods, emotions, and CBT reflections you record in Pebbles may be qualified as special category data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR (data concerning mental health and psychological well-being).

### 4.2 Processing and Consent

This data is processed only:
- On the basis of your explicit consent, given when you create your account;
- With your full control over who accesses it;
- Solely for purposes of personal improvement and memory collection.

### 4.3 Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time from Settings.

Pebbles cannot operate without permission to record how you feel: that processing is the service itself. Withdrawing your consent therefore closes your account and permanently erases everything in it, including your pebbles, photos, souls, collections, glyphs and karma. Glyphs other pebblers bought stay available to them, without your name attached. Withdrawal does not render prior processing unlawful.

Permission to publish your public profile is separate. You can turn it off at any time from Settings without closing your account, and your profile becomes private again immediately.

## 6. International Data Transfers

### 6.1 Storage and Processing in the European Union

Your data is stored by Supabase on servers in Paris (France, EU), and the server-side code that reads it runs in Vercel's Paris region (`cdg1`). Primary storage and processing therefore remain within the European Union.

### 6.2 Transfers to the United States

One form of processing involves the United States.

**Delivery of the application.** Every request to Pebbles is routed by Vercel's global edge network, which terminates TLS, before reaching the Paris region. Your IP address, the address of the page requested and your session cookie therefore transit infrastructure operated from the United States. We rely on Vercel Inc.'s certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

### 6.3 Fonts and Static Assets

Our fonts are self-hosted: no request is made to Google Fonts or an equivalent third-party font service. They are served, like the rest of the application, through the Vercel edge network described in 6.2.

## 7. Data Retention Periods

### 7.1 Account Data

- Kept until you delete your account.
- Erased immediately and permanently when you delete it. There is no grace period, so a deleted account cannot be restored.

### 7.2 Events, Moods, and Responses

- Kept until you delete the pebble or your account, whichever comes first. Nothing deletes them automatically over time.
- Erased immediately when you delete them.

### 7.3 Authentication Logs

- Retained for 12 months for security and compliance reasons.
- Deleted after 12 months.

### 7.4 Cairns

- Computed on your device from your pebbles each time they are displayed.
- Never stored, so there is nothing to retain or delete.

### 7.5 Backups

- Deleted data may persist in our backups for 90 days.
- After 90 days, no trace remains.

## 8. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the following rights:

### 8.1 Right of Access (Art. 15)

You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

### 8.2 Right of Rectification (Art. 16)

You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

### 8.3 Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

You may request deletion of your personal data. Deleting your account from Settings erases your data immediately and permanently, with no grace period, so a deleted account cannot be recovered. Copies held in backups expire as described in Section 7.5.

### 8.4 Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)

You may request that we restrict the processing of your data in certain circumstances.

### 8.5 Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)

You may request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

### 8.6 Right to Object (Art. 21)

You may object to the processing of your data on the basis of legitimate interest.

### 8.7 Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Because the processing of your moods, emotions and reflections is the service itself, withdrawing that consent closes your account and erases your data. Section 4.3 sets out exactly what happens, and covers the separate permission for your public profile, which you can withdraw on its own.

### 8.8 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, send a request to:

**Email :** hello@bohns.design

We will process your request within 30 days. If your request is complex, we may request an extension of 2 additional months.

## 9. Minor Users

### 9.1 Minimum Age

Pebbles is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. Sixteen is the age of consent for information society services under Article 8 of the GDPR, and we apply it uniformly rather than varying it by country.

### 9.2 How We Apply It

We do not ask for your date of birth, and we do not store one. Where we ask you to confirm your age, we keep a record of that confirmation and nothing more.

### 9.3 If We Learn Otherwise

If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we close it and erase the data. A parent or legal guardian who believes their child holds an account can tell us at hello@bohns.design and we will do the same.

## 10. Data Security

### 10.1 Technical Measures

- **Row-Level Security (RLS) :** Each user can only access their own data via Supabase security policies.
- **Authentication :** Secure authentication via Supabase Auth with email and password.
- **Encryption in Transit :** All connections use HTTPS.
- **Encryption at Rest :** Data is encrypted at rest via Supabase infrastructure.

### 10.2 Organizational Measures

- Pebbles is managed by a single developer, Alexis Bohn, who follows security best practices.
- Limited access to data: only the data controller has access to systems.
- No permanent audit or user data logging is retained without necessity.

### 10.3 User Responsibility

You are responsible for keeping your password confidential. Never share your login credentials. If you believe your account is compromised, contact hello@bohns.design immediately.
