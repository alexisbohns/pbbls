# Credits

Pebbles is built on the shoulders of projects, researchers, and creators whose work is generous, rigorous, and often invisible. This document is a way of saying thank you.

---

## Typography

Pebbles ships four typefaces inside the web app and the iOS and Android apps. All four are distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, reproduced in full at the end of this page. No font has been modified, and none is sold on its own.

**Ysabeau** by Christian Thalmann (Catharsis Fonts) — the elegant, humanist family that gives Pebbles its visual voice, in titles and headings.
Copyright 2023 The Ysabeau Project Authors ([github.com/CatharsisFonts/Ysabeau](https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Ysabeau)).

**Inclusive Sans** by Olivia King — a typeface designed for readability, used for every body text and label.
Copyright 2022 The Inclusive Sans Project Authors ([github.com/LivKing/Inclusive-Sans](https://github.com/LivKing/Inclusive-Sans)).

**Caveat** by Impallari Type — a handwriting face for the hand-drawn touches.
Copyright 2014 The Caveat Project Authors ([github.com/googlefonts/caveat](https://github.com/googlefonts/caveat)).

**Reenie Beanie** by James Grieshaber (Typeco) — a second, scribblier handwriting face.
Copyright (c) 2010 Typeco (james@typeco.com). All rights reserved.

## Intellectual inspirations

Pebbles would not exist without the work of the following people, who have deeply influenced the product's design:

**Cognitive science and behavioral psychology:** Daniel Kahneman (thinking systems), Aaron Beck (cognitive behavioral therapy), Abraham Maslow (hierarchy of needs), Lisa Feldman Barrett (emotion construction), Wendy Suzuki (neuroplasticity and habits), Anna Lembke (dopamine and balance).

**Behavioral design and habits:** James Clear (Atomic Habits), BJ Fogg (Tiny Habits), Nir Eyal (Hooked Model), Edward Deci and Richard Ryan (Self-Determination Theory).

**Game design:** Will Wright (The Sims — simulating everyday life), Sid Meier (Civilization — the "one more turn" engagement mechanic).

**Product and methods:** The teams at Linear, Notion, Figma, and Supabase, whose product-building methods are a continuous source of inspiration.

**Applications:** Duolingo (ethical gamification of habits), Polarsteps (turning a trip into a story), Day One (private journaling and personal archive).
